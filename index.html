PDFPick Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-18

PDFPick is local-first. It requires no account, shows no ads, includes no analytics, and operates no PDFPick cloud service.

Files and local data

Selected PDFs and images are processed on device. PDFPick does not intentionally upload documents, form values, text, passwords, images, or signatures. The app requests no INTERNET permission.

History, preferences, and optional saved signatures remain in app-private local storage. Android backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled.

Android services

Read Aloud passes text to the Android TextToSpeech engine selected on the device. That external engine can have its own privacy behavior. Sharing and printing use apps selected by the user.

Advertising and sharing

PDFPick does not sell personal data, share data with advertisers, or contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

Permissions

PDFPick uses per-document and folder URI grants and Photo Picker, not broad storage or media access.
